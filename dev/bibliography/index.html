Bibliography

The manual cites each result where it uses it. This page says, paper by paper, what the package takes from each.

Certificates

PaperWhat the package takes from it
(Ahmadi et al., 2014)path-complete graphs, and the bound on the joint spectral radius: StabilityProblem, is_path_complete, expanded_form
(Philippe et al., 2019)complete and co-complete graphs, and the common function: common
(Anand et al., 2024)path-complete barrier functions: SafetyProblem
(Ninite et al., 2026)path-complete optimal control: OptimalControlProblem
(Abate et al., 2026)the characterization of path-completeness by the subset construction

Templates

PaperWhat the package takes from it
(Athanasopoulos and Jungers, 2019)ParallelFacesPolyhedralTemplate
(Parrilo and Jadbabaie, 2008)SumOfSquaresTemplate
(Debauche, 2024)DualCopositiveTemplate

QuadraticTemplate and LinearCopositiveTemplate are standard. ConicPolyhedralTemplate is not published yet.

Lifts and graphs

PaperWhat the package takes from it
(Athanasopoulos and Jungers, 2019)the partial lifts: OutSplitLift, InSplitLift, ForwardProductLift, BackwardProductLift
(Philippe et al., 2016)PathDependentLift and ProductLift
(Philippe and Jungers, 2019)the order between graphs for every template: simulation
(Debauche et al., 2022)the order for a given template, lifts and their validity: is_valid, MinLift, MaxLift, SumLift
(Debauche et al., 2022)the min and max lifts decided by a relation
(Debauche et al., 2023)the sum lift decided by a linear program, and the graphs of Compare two graphs
(Debauche, 2024)duality, the De Bruijn graphs, and the composition lifts: CompositionLift, BackwardCompositionLift
(Jongeneel and Jungers, 2025)the composition lifts of any length

Refinement

PaperWhat the package takes from it
(Ninite and Jungers, 2026)TightestNodeSplitting, the tight subgraph and is_jsr_exact
(Athanasopoulos and Jungers, 2019)BestOfTightEdgeLifts

Related work

Not implemented here: (Abate et al., 2026) derives the min, max and sum lifts from the algebra of each operation; (Anand et al., 2025) orders barrier certificates; (Wintenberg and Jungers, 2026) orders graphs for templates closed under composition; (Abate et al., 2026) designs the graph and its certificate together with an SMT solver.
