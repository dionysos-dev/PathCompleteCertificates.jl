Bibliography

The manual cites each result where it uses it. This page says, paper by paper, what the package takes from each.

Certificates

PaperWhat the package takes from it
Ahmadi et al. (2014)path-complete graphs, and the bound on the joint spectral radius: StabilityProblem, is_path_complete, expanded_form
Philippe et al. (2019)complete and co-complete graphs, and the common certificate: common
Anand et al. (2024)path-complete barrier certificates: SafetyProblem
Ninite et al. (2026)path-complete optimal control: OptimalControlProblem
Abate et al. (2026)the characterization of path-completeness by the subset construction

Templates

PaperWhat the package takes from it
Athanasopoulos and Jungers (2019)the two polyhedral parameterizations, by pairs of opposite vertices and by pairs of parallel faces, which PiecewiseLinearTemplate and its dual DualPiecewiseLinearTemplate extend to any partition into cones
Parrilo and Jadbabaie (2008)SumOfSquaresTemplate
Debauche et al. (2022)the primal and dual linear copositive norms: LinearCopositiveTemplate and DualCopositiveTemplate

QuadraticTemplate is standard. PiecewiseQuadraticTemplate keeps the cones of PiecewiseLinearTemplate, with a quadratic form on each.

Set programming

Every template is a family of sets of SetProg.jl, and every problem writes its conditions as inclusions between the sets of the node functions, which SetProg.jl reformulates for each family.

PaperWhat the package takes from it
Legat et al. (2019), Legat (2020)SetProg.jl: the set variables, the inclusion constraints and the families on which every template is built
Legat and Jungers (2023)the reformulation of an inclusion for each family, imposed on the sets or on their polars, whichever keeps the family tractable
Gardner et al. (2013)the polar $L_\delta$ sum, with which OptimalControlProblem writes the Bellman inequality as an inclusion
Weisser et al. (2019)SumOfSquares.jl, with which SetProg.jl imposes the conditions of SumOfSquaresTemplate and PiecewiseQuadraticTemplate

Related work

Not implemented here:

  • Abate et al. (2026) derives the min, max and sum lifts from the algebra of each operation.
  • Anand et al. (2025) orders barrier certificates.
  • Wintenberg and Jungers (2026) orders graphs for templates closed under composition.
  • Abate et al. (2026) also designs the graph and its certificate together, with an SMT solver.
  • Legat et al. (2020) certifies the instability of switched systems with sums of squares, in SwitchOnSafety.jl.
  • Raković (2021) and Raković (2025) write the Bellman equation of gauge costs with sets. Summers et al. (2013) relaxes Bellman inequalities with sums of squares for discounted stochastic control, and Rantzer and Johansson (2000) bounds the cost of given control laws with piecewise quadratic functions in continuous time.
