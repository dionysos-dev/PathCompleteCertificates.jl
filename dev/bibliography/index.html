Bibliography

Grouped by what each paper underwrites in the code, since that is usually how you arrive: reading a predicate or a template, wanting its source. Every definition and theorem number quoted in the manual was read in the paper it is attributed to.

The framework

(Ahmadi et al., 2014) introduced path-complete graph Lyapunov functions and the joint-spectral-radius bound they give. Its Definitions 2.1 and 2.2 are the expanded form of a graph with words on its edges, expanded_form, and what path-completeness means for one.

(Philippe et al., 2017) is the authority for Path-complete graphs: Def. II.1 is is_path_complete, Def. III.2 is is_complete and is_co_complete, and Cor. III.3 with Thm. III.8 are what common dispatches on. Its Thm. IV.4 is the linear program of conic_witness.

(Philippe and Jungers, 2019) is the template-free ordering by simulation, simulation: Def. 3.1 defines it and Thm. 3.5 shows it characterises the order that holds for every template.

(Philippe et al., 2016) is constrained switching: the system's automaton as the language a graph must read, decided by is_path_complete(graph, automaton) and seeded by seed. Its Def. 2 is ProductLift and its Def. 3 is PathDependentLift.

Template sources

(Athanasopoulos and Jungers, 2019) is PolyhedralTemplate, the symmetric $2n$-face form, facets fixed, weights solved for.

ConicPolyhedralTemplate, the free-facet form, is not yet published; it is under submission. There is deliberately no entry for it, rather than a citation to the nearest published neighbour that would credit the wrong result.

QuadraticTemplate and LinearCopositiveTemplate are standard and have no single source. DualCopositiveTemplate is Def. 2.30 of (Debauche, 2024).

(Parrilo and Jadbabaie, 2008) is SumOfSquaresTemplate: the sum-of-squares relaxation of the joint spectral radius, whose bound tightens monotonically with the degree.

Problem sources

(Anand et al., 2024) is SafetyProblem. (Anand et al., 2025) is its follow-up on ordering barriers, not implemented here.

(Ninite et al., 2026) is OptimalControlProblem, including the $S = P^{-1}$, $Y = KS$ substitution.

StabilityProblem is (Ahmadi et al., 2014) directly.

Sources for lifts and duality

(Debauche et al., 2022) defines a lift as any map on graphs preserving path-completeness (AbstractLift, its Def. 5), validity as the guarantee of no worse a bound (is_valid, Def. 6), and the sum, min, max and composition lifts (SumLift, MinLift, MaxLift, CompositionLift; Defs. 8, 9 and 10).

(Debauche, 2024) supplies the rest of Duality: the dual graph (Def. 6.3), the dual certificate (Lemma 6.25), the dual lift (Def. 7.4) and its validity (Prop. 7.5), the max lift as the dual of the min lift (Lemma 7.45), the backward composition lift (Def. 7.61) as the dual of the forward one (Prop. 7.64), and the validity of the min lift for the copositive template without closure (Thm. 7.43).

(Athanasopoulos and Jungers, 2019) gives the four partial lifts, ForwardEdgeSplit, BackwardEdgeSplit, ForwardEdgeProduct and BackwardEdgeProduct, Defs. 4 to 7.

(Jongeneel and Jungers, 2025) is the T-fold CompositionLift, Def. III.1, and the reason it needs invertible dynamics.

(Abate et al., 2026) rederives the min, max and sum lifts from the algebraic theory of each operation; its Example III.23 relates its sum lift to SumLift. (Wintenberg and Jungers, 2026) corrects the composition lifts and is not yet available.

Sources for comparing graphs

(Debauche et al., 2023) proves the linear program complete for templates closed under addition, Thm. 2, and its five graphs are Comparing graphs: which one is better?.

(Debauche, 2024) characterises the order for min-closed and max-closed templates by the simulation of the min and max lifts (Thms. 8.1 and 8.5), which simulation_relation decides without building them, and shows that complete graphs buy nothing over one node for min-closed templates (Cor. 7.53), which is simplify.

Sources for refinement

(Ninite and Jungers, 2026) is refine: the forward lift at a node (Def. 3), the tight subgraph (Def. 4), and the optimality certificate is_jsr_exact (Thm. 4).
