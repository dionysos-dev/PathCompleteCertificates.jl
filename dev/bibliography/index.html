Bibliography

What the package takes from each paper. Every definition and theorem number quoted in the manual was read in the paper it is attributed to.

Path-complete graphs and stability

(Ahmadi et al., 2014)path-complete graph Lyapunov functions and the bound on the joint spectral radius: StabilityProblem, is_path_complete, expanded_form
(Philippe et al., 2017)complete and co-complete graphs, is_complete and is_co_complete, the common function each licenses, common, and the linear program of SumLift()
(Philippe et al., 2016)common_graph, ProductLift and PathDependentLift, and the backward split that takes the De Bruijn hierarchy one order up

The templates

(Athanasopoulos and Jungers, 2019)ParallelFacesPolyhedralTemplate, fixed facets and solved-for weights, and the four partial lifts that are OutSplitLift, InSplitLift, ForwardProductLift and BackwardProductLift
(Parrilo and Jadbabaie, 2008)SumOfSquaresTemplate
(Debauche, 2024)DualCopositiveTemplate, Def. 2.30

QuadraticTemplate and LinearCopositiveTemplate are standard. ConicPolyhedralTemplate is under submission and has no entry yet.

Safety and optimal control

(Anand et al., 2024)SafetyProblem, path-complete barrier functions
(Ninite et al., 2026)OptimalControlProblem, including the substitution that makes it convex

Comparing graphs

(Philippe and Jungers, 2019)the order that holds for every template, decided by a simulation
(Debauche et al., 2022)the order $G \le_V H$, lifts and their validity, is_valid, and the SumLift, MinLift, MaxLift and CompositionLift
(Debauche et al., 2022)the min and max lifts decided by a simulation relation, without building them
(Debauche et al., 2023)the linear program of the sum lift proved complete, and the five graphs of Comparing graphs: five graphs, three templates
(Debauche, 2024)Duality throughout: the dual graph, system, template, certificate and lift; the De Bruijn graphs; BackwardCompositionLift; the validity of the min lift for the copositive template
(Jongeneel and Jungers, 2025)the $T$-fold CompositionLift, the counterexample to its completeness, and its use as a step of refine

Refining a graph

(Ninite and Jungers, 2026)TightestNodeSplitting, the default algorithm of refine; the forward lift at a node, OutSplitLift; the tight subgraph, tight_subgraph, and the optimality certificate is_jsr_exact
(Athanasopoulos and Jungers, 2019)BestOfTightEdgeLifts, the procedure of its Example 2

The lifts themselves come from (Athanasopoulos and Jungers, 2019) and (Philippe et al., 2016), listed above.

Related work

(Abate et al., 2026) rederives the min, max and sum lifts from the algebra of each operation. (Anand et al., 2025) orders barrier certificates, and (Wintenberg and Jungers, 2026) gives a complete ordering for composition-closed templates, where the composition lift is sufficient only. (Abate et al., 2026) designs the graph and its certificate together with an SMT solver. None of these is implemented here.
