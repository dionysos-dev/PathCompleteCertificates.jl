PathCompleteCertificates.jl

Certificates of stability, safety and optimal control for switched systems in the path-complete framework. A certificate consists of a graph and a template, both chosen by the user. The package solves for a certificate on a given graph and template, compares two graphs, and refines a graph using its certificate.

Path-complete certificates

A switched linear system evolves according to $x_{k+1} = A_{\sigma(k)}\, x_k$, where $\sigma$ is a switching signal taking values in a finite set of modes, under arbitrary switching. A certificate must hold for every switching sequence. In the path-complete framework ((Ahmadi et al., 2014)) a certificate is specified by three ingredients:

  1. a labelled graph whose labels are the modes;
  2. a function $V_s$ at each node, drawn from a template;
  3. one inequality along each edge, determined by the problem.

When the graph is path-complete, meaning that every admissible switching sequence can be read as a path in it, these local inequalities imply a property of the whole system. The three problems differ in the edge inequality and in the conditions on the node functions:

ProblemEdge inequality on $(s, d, i)$Conditions on the node functionsThe certificate carries
StabilityProblem$V_d(A_i x) \le \gamma^{p}\, V_s(x)$positive definite, homogeneous of degree $p$rate, an upper bound on the joint spectral radius
SafetyProblem$B_d(A_i x) \le B_s(x)$negative on the initial set, positive on the unsafe setmargin, how strictly the two sets are separated
OptimalControlProblem$x^\top Q x + u^\top R u + V_d(A_i x + B_i u) \le V_s(x)$positive definite quadraticgains, a feedback per node, and the bound $V$ on the cost

What you can do

TakesGives
Certifya template, a graph and a problema certificate: the node functions, what the problem certifies (rate, margin or gains), and the common function, evaluated by calling the certificate c as c(x)
Buildan order and an alphabet, or a systemDe Bruijn graphs and their duals, the common Lyapunov function graph, graphs with words on their edges
Comparetwo graphs, and optionally templates and a systemwhether the second graph is no worse than the first, for which templates, and the simulation that proves it
Refinea template, a graph and a problema sequence of graphs, each refined where its certificate is tight, until the bound is proved exact or a budget is reached

Safety and optimal control have one example each, Safety: a barrier certificate and Optimal control: a feedback per node, and so does the SetProg template, Piecewise semi-ellipsoids: a template from SetProg.

Scope

Stability is implemented for every template, safety and optimal control for the quadratic one. compare is stated for stability, as in the literature, and refine runs on stability and safety; What works with what gives the full table. A bound is always an upper bound, and a negative answer means that no certificate was found in this template on this graph, not that the property fails.

Other tools

SwitchOnSafety.jl and the MATLAB JSR Toolbox compute bounds on the joint spectral radius by several methods, some of which use path-complete graphs. This package is restricted to path-complete certificates, and adds the comparison and refinement of their graphs.
