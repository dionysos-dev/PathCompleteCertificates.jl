Templates

A template defines the search space from which the node functions of a certificate are chosen. Its properties depend on the problem: for stability, the template consists of candidate Lyapunov functions, continuous, positive definite and homogeneous; for safety, of candidate barrier functions, which need not be positive. Once a certificate has been computed, functions returns the fitted node functions, one per node, each callable.

The homogeneity degree, homogeneity_degree, is the $p$ in $V(cx) = c^p V(x)$. Stability scales its edge inequality by $\gamma^p$, so the rate bounds the joint spectral radius in every template, and rates from different templates can be compared. For safety the quadratic template is used in homogeneous coordinates, $[x; 1]^\top P_s [x; 1]$, without positive definiteness.

The two polyhedral templates. Both search symmetric polytopes with a linear program. ParallelFacesPolyhedralTemplate fixes the face normals, the rows of $G_s$, and solves for the distances $w_s$ of the faces, $n$ variables per node. ConicPolyhedralTemplate fixes the vertex directions, the rays of a conic partition, and solves for the faces, one row of $n$ variables per cone.

The two copositive templates are each other's duals, the dual norm of $c^\top x$ on the orthant being $\max_i x_i / c_i$. Both are defined on the nonnegative orthant, so they apply to positive systems only: on any other, is_applicable is false and stability throws.

Classes and solvers

Closure properties

A lift is valid for a template, guaranteed not to worsen the bound, when the template is closed under the operation the lift combines node functions with ((Debauche et al., 2022), Defs. 6 and 7): the sum lift needs closure under addition, the min and max lifts under pointwise minimum and maximum, the composition lifts under composition with the dynamics. A result of compare holds for a template when the lift that gave it is valid for the template. is_closed_under returns the closures of a template:

Closure is a property of the template as implemented. Polyhedral norms are closed under maximum, but the maximum of two members of the parallel-faces template has the union of their facets, so that template is closed only when every node has the same facet matrix. The free-facet template has a fixed number of facets and declares no closure.

Dual templates

has_dual tests whether a template has a dual, dual(template) returns it, and dual(template, V) returns the dual norm of a fitted function V. Quadratic forms are self-dual and the copositive templates are each other's dual; the polyhedral, sum-of-squares and SetProg templates have none here. A closure of one template of a dual pair is used for both; see Duality.

Per-node data

The facet matrices of ParallelFacesPolyhedralTemplate and the set variables of SetProgTemplate are attached to the nodes. When a refinement copies a node, reindex(template, origins) gives the copy the data of the node it came from.

Writing your own

A new template implements the primitives listed in the developer conventions: its variables, the edge inequality, and how a solution is read back. The problems need no change.
