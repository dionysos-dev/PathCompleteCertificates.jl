Duality

Reversing every edge of the graph, transposing every mode matrix and replacing every node function by its dual norm turns a certificate into a certificate at the same rate. dual applies this operation to each object of the package, and every backward construction is the dual of a forward one.

What dual does to each object

Objectdual of itSource
graphevery edge and every word reversed; path-complete exactly when the original is(Debauche, 2024), Def. 6.3 and Prop. 6.6
systemevery mode matrix transposed(Debauche, 2024), Lemma 6.25
templatethe family of dual norms: quadratic forms are their own, the two copositive templates each other's(Debauche, 2024), Def. 1.32
node functiondual(template, V), the dual norm of a fitted function; it satisfies the reversed edge inequality(Debauche, 2024), Lemma 1.28
certificatethe dual norms on the dual graph for the dual system, at the same rate, nothing solved(Debauche, 2024), Lemmas 6.25 and 6.26
lift, refinementdual(L)(G) = dual(L(dual(G))); a lift's simulation becomes the same on the dual graphs(Debauche, 2024), Def. 7.4
operationwhat the dual lift requires: min and max exchange, + is fixed, composition becomes composition with the inverse(Debauche, 2024), Lemma 7.45, Prop. 7.13 and Prop. 7.64

dual is an involution on each: applied twice it gives the object back, down to the node functions of a certificate. For quadratic forms this is the classical statement that transposing the matrices and dualising the graph give the same bound ((Ahmadi et al., 2014), Thm. 5.1).

Consequences

  • Backward constructions. BackwardEdgeSplit, BackwardEdgeProduct, MaxLift and BackwardCompositionLift are dual of their forward counterparts, and the sum and product constructions are self-dual.
  • Validity of lifts. A lift valid for a template has a dual valid for the dual template ((Debauche, 2024), Prop. 7.5), so is_valid also reads the closures of the dual template: the sum lift is valid for the dual copositive template because the linear copositive template is closed under addition.
  • Order. $G \le_V H$ exactly when $\mathrm{dual}(G) \le_{V^*} \mathrm{dual}(H)$ ((Debauche, 2024), Prop. 6.27), and MaxLift decides the max-closed case by a relation on the dual graphs.
  • A check of a certificate. dual(certificate) is computed without a solve. Its reversed edge inequalities hold by edge_slacks, a solve of the dual problem returns the same rate, and applying dual again returns the node functions.

Templates without a dual here, the polyhedral and sum-of-squares ones, answer has_dual with false, and is_valid reads only their own closures.
