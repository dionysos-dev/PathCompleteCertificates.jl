Bibliography

Grouped by what each paper underwrites in the code, since that is usually how you arrive: reading a predicate or a template, wanting its source.

The framework

(Ahmadi et al., 2014) introduced path-complete graph Lyapunov functions and the joint-spectral-radius bound they give. The package is an implementation of it.

(Philippe et al., 2017) is the authority for Path-complete graphs: Def. II.1 is is_path_complete, Def. III.2 is is_complete and is_co_complete, and Cor. III.3 / Thm III.8 are what common dispatches on.

Template sources

(Athanasopoulos and Jungers, 2019) is PolyhedralTemplate — the symmetric $2n$-face form, facets fixed, weights solved for.

ConicPolyhedralTemplate, the free-facet form, is not yet published; it is under submission. There is deliberately no entry for it, rather than a citation to the nearest published neighbour that would credit the wrong result.

QuadraticTemplate and LinearCopositiveTemplate are standard and have no single source.

Problem sources

(Anand et al., 2024) is SafetyProblem. (Anand et al., 2025) is its follow-up on ordering barriers, not implemented here.

(Ninite et al., 2026) is OptimalControlProblem, including the $S = P^{-1}$, $Y = KS$ substitution.

StabilityProblem is (Ahmadi et al., 2014) directly.

Designed, not implemented

Both describe traps that fail silently.

(Debauche et al., 2021) — whether a lift may be applied depends on the template's analytical properties, not on the graph alone. Skip that check and you get a certificate that certifies nothing.

(Jongeneel and Jungers, 2025) — ordering and refining path-complete Lyapunov functions through composition lifts. The work named in Status.
